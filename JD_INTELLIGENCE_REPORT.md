# Job Description Intelligence Analysis

## 1. Job Title
Senior AI Engineer — Founding Team

## 2. Seniority Level
Senior level. 
*Expectation:* While 5–9 years of total experience is the benchmark, the actual requirement is "senior engineer judgment." They are looking for roughly 6-8 years total experience, with 4-5 years specifically in applied ML/AI.

## 3. Mandatory Skills
- **Retrieval Systems:** Production experience with embeddings-based retrieval systems (sentence-transformers, OpenAI embeddings, BGE, E5, etc.) and handling embedding drift / index refreshes.
- **Vector Databases / Search Infra:** Production experience with Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant, Milvus, OpenSearch, Elasticsearch, or FAISS.
- **Evaluation Frameworks:** Hands-on experience designing offline/online evaluation frameworks for ranking systems (NDCG, MRR, MAP, A/B testing).
- **Programming:** Strong Python skills and care for code quality.

## 4. Preferred Skills
- LLM fine-tuning (LoRA, QLoRA, PEFT).
- Learning-to-rank (LTR) models (XGBoost-based or neural networks).
- Distributed systems or large-scale inference optimization.
- Open-source contributions in the AI/ML space.

## 5. Core Technologies
- Python
- Vector Databases (Pinecone, Qdrant, Milvus, FAISS, etc.)
- Embedding Models (BGE, E5, sentence-transformers)
- Evaluation Metrics Tooling (NDCG, MRR, MAP)

## 6. Domain Expertise Requirements
- **NLP / Information Retrieval (IR):** Must have deep expertise in natural language processing, semantic search, and retrieval.
- **Product Domain:** HR-tech, recruiting tech, or marketplace matching is highly preferred.
- **Strict Exclusions:** Candidates whose primary domain is Computer Vision, Speech, or Robotics (without significant NLP/IR exposure) are explicitly disqualified.

## 7. Years of Experience Expectations
- **Stated:** 5–9 years.
- **Actual / Ideal:** 6–8 years total experience, with 4–5 years in applied ML/AI roles at *product* companies (not IT services).

## 8. Leadership Expectations
- **Mentorship & Architecture:** Mentoring the next round of hires (growing the team from 4 to 12 engineers) and driving the long-term architecture of candidate-JD matching.
- **Hands-on Ownership:** Must "own the intelligence layer." However, candidates who have transitioned entirely into "architecture" or "tech lead" roles and haven't written production code in the last 18 months will be rejected. This role writes code.

## 9. AI/ML Expectations
- **Dual Mindset ("Researcher" + "Shipper"):** Needs deep technical depth in modern ML systems (embeddings, retrieval, LLMs) but must possess a scrappy, product-engineering attitude (willing to ship a sub-optimal "v1" in a week to learn from users).
- **Systems over Frameworks:** They want engineers who understand retrieval and ranking fundamentals, not just "LangChain wrapper" engineers who started doing AI 12 months ago.
- **Applied over Theoretical:** Pure research experience (academic labs, research-only roles) without real production deployment to users is a disqualifier.

## 10. Hidden Signals (Recruiter Inferences)
- **Tenure Stability:** "Title-chasers" who switch companies every 1.5 years to get a "Staff" or "Principal" title are disqualified. They want a 3+ year commitment.
- **Company DNA:** Product company experience is mandatory. Candidates whose entire career is in pure consulting/services (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture) will be rejected unless they also have product experience.
- **Authenticity & Passion:** Open-source contributions, public talks, or papers are strong signals. They don't want engineers whose work is entirely locked behind closed-source proprietary systems for 5+ years without external validation.
- **Logistical Constraints:** Notice period of <30 days is strongly preferred. 30+ days raises the bar significantly. Location in Pune/Noida is preferred (hybrid).

- **Experience & DNA Weight = 30%** 
  *(Product company background, no pure services, no pure research, stable tenure > 1.5 yrs).*
- **Skill Match Weight = 25%** 
  *(Embeddings, Vector Search, FAISS, Pinecone, Evaluation frameworks, Python, NLP/IR. Excludes CV/Robotics).*
- **Execution / Builder Signals Weight = 20%**

  *(Notice period <30 days, recent login activity, high message response rate, interview completion history).*

  *(LLM Fine-tuning, HR-tech/Marketplace experience).*

## "If an expert recruiter manually reviewed 100,000 candidates for this JD, what exact characteristics would make a candidate rank in the top 10?"

To reach the absolute **Top 10** out of 100,000, a candidate must possess a near-perfect intersection of Deep Tech, Builder DNA, and Real-World Availability. The exact profile would look like this:

1. **The Title & Pedigree:** Current title is "Senior Machine Learning Engineer", "Search Engineer", or "AI Engineer" at a known, high-growth **product company** or startup (not an IT consultancy like TCS/Infosys, and not a pure research lab).
2. **The Tenure:** They have been at their current company for at least 2.5 to 3 years, proving they stay long enough to maintain the systems they build.
3. **The Work (Semantic Alignment):** Their resume summary or role descriptions explicitly mention building and deploying **search, retrieval, or recommendation systems** using **Vector Databases** (Pinecone, Qdrant, Milvus) and evaluating them with **NDCG/MRR**. They do not just list 50 AI buzzwords; they describe the *scale* of what they shipped.
4. **The Red Flags (Absent):** They do NOT list "Computer Vision" or "Robotics" as their primary focus. They do NOT solely rely on "LangChain/OpenAI" projects from the last 8 months.
5. **The Behavioral Goldmine:** 
   - They have a **sub-30 day notice period** (or are immediately available).
   - They reside in **Pune or Noida** (or state explicit willingness to relocate).
   - They have logged into the platform within the last 72 hours.
   - They have a **>80% recruiter response rate**, proving they are actively looking and communicative.
6. **The Clincher:** They have a strong **GitHub activity score** or explicit open-source ML contributions, validating they are a hands-on "shipper."
